During the cycle pause, tracks stay at zero and the mast holds its last target. STOP, Escape, leaving the page, stale telemetry, a robot restart, rejected commands or a delayed control loop end playback. Reconnecting never restarts it.


Rehearse at the van
Each cycle is at most 90 seconds, with up to 5 seconds of track commands. Track cues are limited to 25% and 1.5 seconds each. Start with tracks raised clear, then tune short pulses on the actual floor. Timed moves can drift on every repeat; reversing does not guarantee a return to the starting position. Servo OFF releases holding torque.
